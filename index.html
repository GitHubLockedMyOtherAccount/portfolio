About me

Andrew Adams Communications Specialist

I help game and tech startups talk to their players. Since 2019 I've grown Discord communities from a few thousand people to over 150,000, run more than a thousand live tournaments, and produced 60+ videos and three animated shorts.

Startups rarely have a person for every job, so I've learned to be several of them: community manager, writer, marketer, producer, tournament organizer and director of sales. I've written every player email, web page and patch note for a live game, planned the content calendar that grew its YouTube channel, and run a live broadcast from the director's chair.

Behind the scenes I keep teams moving. I've led weekly goals and retrospectives across engineering, art, QA and community, and I'm at my best when plans change overnight and someone has to make the call and tell everyone what happens next.
